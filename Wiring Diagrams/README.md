# Wiring Diagrams

This directory contains two wiring diagrams for GPT On Wheels, an autonomous AI robot.

## Guide

There are two wiring diagrams:

**1. Prototype (what I have right now)**

**2. Final (once I get the remaining components)**

### Prototype

This is what I currently have working. *The diagram depicts mechanum wheels but in reality I only have normal wheels*. The only good part on Crikit with a wheel and motor the 
way I wanted was this. Also the battery brand inside the 18650s are not UltraFire. Otherwise, the diagram is accurate.

### Final

This is what I will build once I receive the components. *The biggest fault in this is the mechanum wheels, the front left and back right should be a different mechanum wheel
It should be the left variation of the mechanum wheels (with the rollers the opposite direction) in the end the rollers should point an X.* This is impossible to replicate
in Crikit because flipping the component does not rotate the postioning of the rollers themselves. Another detial is that the purple wires represent SDA and the pink wires
represent SCL.

## Other
