## Pan-Tilt

One servo rotates in the x direction and one servo roatates in the y direction.
This is one that I assembled using 3d printed parts (from a previous project):

<img width="1260" height="945" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/27ac6d6a-e40b-4976-b3a3-67e81cad2a1c" />

For GPT On Wheels, I will probably use stronger tape or screws to better hold the parts in place, especially the foam.

## Why it's necessary

Using a pan-tilt the camera can view so much more than just at one static angle. It allows the camera to turn and go up and down, leading to lots of more visiblity.
